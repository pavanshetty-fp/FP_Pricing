## Decisions from 2026-10-07 that must be KEPT

Nothing below proposes undoing these.

- **Sales: five stages** — Requested · Scheduled · Dispatched · Delivered ·
  Cancelled (caution · info · info · success · neutral).
- **Sales Orders is one page** — fuel + asset orders, Line of Service segment
  (All · Fuel Orders · Asset Orders), create menu (Fuel Order · Asset Order ·
  Quote). Sales opens on Orders, Dashboard first.
- **Quotes** — Draft → Send quote → Sent → Mark as accepted → Accepted (makes a
  Requested order); Expired derived; pricing as an editable table with the unit
  in the Value cell; **Save as Draft + Send quote** in the footer (allowed: a
  draft's footer may carry Save as Draft); shared SummaryCard + ActivityCard.
- **Sales Settings tables** — full width, every cell editable, checkboxes,
  delete column on hover, a handle on every reorderable row.
- **ConfiguredForm wiring** — order, sections, labels, visible, required
  **blocks Create**, extra fields stored.
- **Sales Orders alert banner** ("N urgent orders overdue for dispatch") with
  snooze and dismiss, and the **bulk bar**.

### Where Sales now leads 3PL — should 3PL adopt?

| Sales has | 3PL today | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Checkbox tables, delete on hover, handles (Settings) | 3PL has no settings tables; its editable tables already use checkboxes (`LoadOrderTable.tsx:77`) and `TableRowRemove` reveals on hover | **Already in line** — nothing to adopt. |
| Alert banner + snooze/dismiss (G17) | none on Delivery Orders | **Decide.** Candidate: orders whose window has passed while still in Orders/Quoting. Do not build until Pavan names the condition. |
| Bulk bar on orders (G18) | Delivery Orders has no row selection (Directory does) | **Decide.** Candidate acts: invite vendors, cancel. |
| Title-only toasts (G49) | 19 toasts with descriptions in `BrokerOrderSheet.tsx` alone | **Decide the rule first** — it contradicts `3-interactions.md` §2. |
