Branch fee-rule-engine, as built on 2026-11-25. Spec: fee-rule-engine.md. Each case reads Given / do → expect. Use them to test the prototype and to brief dev.

Where things are

  • Pricing setup › Fee: the fee list and the fee sheet.
  • Ship-to configuration: the Fee cell, which opens the fee side sheet.

  • Edit pricing (ship-to) / Customer pricing: the Fee block.
  • Order well (below Dashboard) and Order trace: Order details.

1. The model in one line

Two layers:

  • Configuration (Pricing setup › Fee) decides a fee's value, scope (who it reaches) and application (when and how it's charged).
  • Application (customer / ship-to pricing) can only Remove a fee or Put it back.

Changes reach new and undelivered orders. Delivered orders keep what they had.

14. Adding a fee from a customer or ship-to (decided 2026-11-25: stays on the fee's scope)

Decision (Yash, 2026-11-25): adding stays on the fee's configuration. To give a customer or ship-to a fee, widen the fee's scope. The options below are kept for the record.

Problem. Pricing pages can only remove a fee. A user on a ship-to who wants a fee that doesn't reach it has to leave, find the fee and widen its scope. Customers will ask to add it where they are.

Options

A. Add widens the fee's scope. Picking a fee on a ship-to adds an Include row for that ship-to to the fee's scope.

ProsCons
Scope stays the only source of truthCan't say "this ship-to, propane only". Customer and product scope are separate tables, so the ship-to gets every product the fee covers
Nothing new to learn on the fee pageExclude wins, so if the customer is excluded the Include does nothing unless we change that rule
Scopes fill with one-off rows nobody remembers adding
A pricing-page user edits a fee that may reach thousands of prices

B. "Added here", the mirror of Remove (recommended). Store "also here" on the price or customer, the way Remove stores "not here". The fee's scope is untouched.

ProsCons
Symmetric: Remove / Put back already exists, Add / Undo add fits the same ideaTwo places decide reach: scope + additions / removals
Works per ship-to × productAdditions are invisible on the fee unless we show them
Gets past an exclusion without changing the ruleOne more state to explain in History and Activity
Bulk (phase 2) can write the same additions

C. Add sends you to the fee with the scope pre-filled.

ProsCons
One source of truth; you see the whole fee before savingIt's the "go over there" we want to avoid
No new stateSame per-product and exclusion problems as A

D. Ask each time: "Just here" (B) or "Everywhere for this customer / ship-to" (A).

ProsCons
FlexibleA decision most users can't make well, every time
Carries the cons of both A and B

Recommendation: B, with two guard rails

  1. Show additions on the fee. Reach card: "742 prices · 3 added on pricing pages", with the list one click away. A "Move into scope" act can come later.
  2. An added fee keeps its own application. An added By rule fee charges only when its rule matches. An added Review and apply fee is still offered for review. Add changes where, never how.

How B would work

  • Add fee on the ship-to fee sheet and the Edit pricing Fee block opens a

searchable picker. It lists active fees not reaching this price, with rate and application. Removed fees aren't listed; they have Put back.

  • Confirm: "Add Hazmat fee to Propane HD-5 at Creole Batch Plant? It's charged

on orders here from now on; the fee's scope doesn't change. You can undo it any time."

  • The fee shows in its normal group with an Added here tag. ⋯ offers

Undo add · Edit fee.

  • On a customer: reaches every price following the customer. Ship-tos on

their own pricing keep their own setup (same as Remove).

  • Logged in the fee's History, the price's History and, later, the Activity

feed.

Edge cases

CaseProposed behaviour
Added on the customer, removed on one of its ship-tosShip-to wins (more specific)
Fee's scope later widens to cover an added priceThe addition is redundant: drop it automatically or flag it on the fee
Fee deactivatedStops everywhere, additions included
Ship-to overridden after a customer additionKeeps a snapshot, as with removals
Reset pricing on a ship-toDrops its own additions too
Undelivered orders when a fee is addedPick it up; delivered orders don't
Product not in the fee's product scopeAllowed. The addition is per price, so product scope doesn't block it

This reverses the earlier decision "no hand-adding outside scope". It needs agreement before it's built.

15. Questions to clarify

Add fee (§14)

  1. ~~Go with B ("Added here")?~~ Decided: adding stays on the fee's scope.
  2. Who can add a fee outside its scope? It affects billing, so should it be limited to some roles or need approval?
  3. When the scope later covers an added price, drop the addition silently or flag it on the fee?
  4. Should an addition get past an Exclude row in the scope?
  5. Do we need "Move into scope" on the fee in v1, or later?

Application and rules

  1. Bring back By rule + review (e.g. Rush offered only when it matches)? Today it's three options with no review switch.
  2. Conflicts: "bigger fee wins". Between which fees? Same code, same category, or any two fees doing the same job (Weekend vs Holiday)?
  3. A rule fee that matched before delivery but not at delivery is dropped. Should the order show that it was dropped, and why?

Amounts

  1. Vendor rates for range and % fees, or flat $ only?
  2. What does "%" of total include: product only, or product + other fees?

Hub

  1. Should Hub pricing edits change the prices of ship-tos tagged Hub, and be logged in Activity?
  2. Can a fee be removed at hub level, or only customer / ship-to? (Today only customer / ship-to.)

Screens

  1. Fee groups: keep Tabs or Filter?
  2. Fee cell: show the group counts (e.g. "2 applied · 1 to review") instead of "N fees"?
  3. "New customers, hubs and ship-tos get this fee": show as a visible line or an ⓘ (UX-96)? This one is for Pavan.

Orders

  1. Can a person remove an Always applied fee on a single order, or only on the price?
  2. Invoiced orders: frozen for every fee change, including Put back?
